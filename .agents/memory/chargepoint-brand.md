description: Official brand orange and accessible use on authentication controls.

ChargePoint’s official orange is **#FF7A14**, according to its [2026 brand guidelines](https://www.chargepoint.com/download-file/chargepoint-brand-guidelines).

**Why:** A generic dark orange was initially used for button contrast, but the user specifically requested ChargePoint’s real orange. White text does not provide sufficient contrast against the official hue.

**How to apply:** Use the official color for ChargePoint-themed accents and buttons; pair filled orange buttons with dark text and a dark border for readability. Do not assume the whole legacy app is rebranded until the user specifies the scope.
