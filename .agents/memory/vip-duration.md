---
name: VIP duration
description: Business rule for the duration and total return of VIP products.
---

All VIP products use a 200-day cycle. Their total return must stay consistent with that duration: daily earnings multiplied by 200. The 4,500 FCFA VIP pays 380 FCFA per day, VIP 2 costs 6,000 FCFA and pays 1,500 FCFA per day, and VIP 3 costs 10,000 FCFA and pays 2,568 FCFA per day.

**Why:** The user requested that VIP duration be 200 days.

**How to apply:** Use 200 days for newly seeded VIP products and keep existing VIP total returns synchronized when migrating their cycle duration.
