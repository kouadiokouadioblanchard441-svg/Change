---
name: Demo referrals
description: How to treat fictitious referrals and financial-looking activity examples
---

When a user explicitly requests fictitious referrals saved to their real account, treat them as demonstration records: clearly label them, prevent sign-in and direct contact with invented numbers, and do not grant balances, purchases, commissions, or signup bonuses. Their presence still changes team-member counts. Realistic VIP, purchase, and bonus examples may be shown as display-only previews, never as financial ledger records. A WhatsApp preview may open a prefilled message without a recipient. Likewise, home-page snippets about masked numbers and bonus or withdrawal amounts are illustrative only: label them as fictitious, and never connect them to actual balances or payout events.

**Why:** The user chose persistent test accounts over a temporary preview, then requested realistic numbers and a visible WhatsApp action. These accounts share the actual referral database; simulated financial activity or a link addressed to an invented number would mislead users or potentially contact unrelated people. The user also requested only rotating examples on home, not claims about genuine transactions.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction whenever referral displays, earnings calculations, activity announcements, contact actions, or cleanup of demo members change. Keep genuine financial totals separate from clearly marked examples, and never treat these records as genuine paying referrals.

If a user asks to remove fictitious/demo labels from a rotating activity banner, replace its invented financial events with neutral app messages or verified events; never leave invented payouts looking like real activity. This does not automatically authorize hiding or deleting separate demo referrals on the Team pages.
