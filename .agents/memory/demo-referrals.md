---
name: Demo referrals
description: How to treat persistent fictitious referrals on a real account
---

When a user explicitly requests fictitious referrals saved to their real account, treat them as demonstration records: clearly label them, prevent sign-in and contact actions, and do not grant balances, purchases, commissions, or signup bonuses. Their presence still changes team-member counts.

**Why:** The user chose persistent test accounts over a temporary preview, but those accounts share the actual referral database. Simulated financial activity or a working contact link would mislead users or potentially contact unrelated people.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction whenever referral displays, earnings calculations, contact actions, or cleanup of demo members change. Do not treat these records as genuine paying referrals.
