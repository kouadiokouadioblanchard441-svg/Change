---
name: Demo referrals
description: How to treat persistent fictitious referrals on a real account
---

When a user explicitly requests fictitious referrals saved to their real account, treat them as demonstration records: clearly label them, prevent sign-in and direct contact with invented numbers, and do not grant balances, purchases, commissions, or signup bonuses. Their presence still changes team-member counts. Realistic VIP, purchase, and bonus examples may be shown as display-only previews, never as financial ledger records. A WhatsApp preview may open a prefilled message without a recipient.

**Why:** The user chose persistent test accounts over a temporary preview, then requested realistic numbers and a visible WhatsApp action. These accounts share the actual referral database; simulated financial activity or a link addressed to an invented number would mislead users or potentially contact unrelated people.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction whenever referral displays, earnings calculations, contact actions, or cleanup of demo members change. Keep genuine financial totals separate from clearly marked examples, and never treat these records as genuine paying referrals.
