---
name: Page branding scope
description: Scope of ChargePoint orange versus the legacy TON green styling.
---

The previous TON green refresh is legacy styling on pages not yet rebranded. New pages redesigned from user-provided mockups use ChargePoint orange, including authentication and the home dashboard. Keep untouched pages in their existing styling; do not perform a global recolor.

**Why:** The user previously requested TON green outside the dashboard, but subsequently chose ChargePoint as the new company. They explicitly requested a ChargePoint version of a supplied home-page screenshot with a pure white background, superseding the earlier request to preserve the old dashboard design.

**How to apply:** Use ChargePoint orange for newly requested redesigns, not a global theme change. Preserve older screens until explicitly requested.
